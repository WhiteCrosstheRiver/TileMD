# M7 — Tensor-core pair tile, CPU prototype

## What is in

`reference/tf32_pair.cpp` rounds binary32 to TF32 (10-bit mantissa, nearest even) and evaluates the M7 score `A · B = |r_i - r_j|^2` three ways: FP32, plain TF32, and 3×TF32. The force prototype takes `s` from the 3×TF32 distance and the direction from the double-precision minimum-image vector. The engine does not call it. There is no `mma.sync` kernel and no CUTLASS fragment.

## What was run

`BUILD_DIR=build-m7 bash scripts/check.sh` passes the CPU suite. The numbers below are printed by `tilemd_cpu_tests --gtest_filter=*Tf32*`. `docs/NUMERICS.md` records the same runs.

On pairs sitting far from the origin, plain TF32 has relative `r^2` error 0.384. The same pairs about their centroid drop that to `5.5e-4`, and 3×TF32 drops it further to `2.2e-7`. A compact 32-atom cloud gives a 3×TF32 force relative error of `9.9e-7`, inside the T1 bound of `1e-5`.

On the fixtures the tile origin is the centroid of the current 8-atom z-slice. That slice is long, so the cancellation factor stays above 1. The 4000-atom melt then has a 3×TF32 force relative error of `7.9e-5`, about eight times T1. `cutoff_500` is `1.1e-5`. Fixtures with a near contact (`boundary_500`, `dense_2000`, `twotype_2000`, `dilute_1000`) are `1e-3` to `19`, because `s(D)` is singular and the slice center does not sit on that pair. In every fixture, 3×TF32 still beats plain TF32 on pairs with `d^2 > 0.25`.

## Gate

A second measurement replaces the z-slice origin with the centroid of a cubic cell sized for about 4, 8, or 16 atoms. On `cutoff_500` the force relative error drops to `3.2e-6`. On `melt_4000` the 4-atom cell reaches `1.9e-5` and the 16-atom cell `3.9e-5`. Both stay outside T1. Shortening the tile removes part of the z-slice error and leaves the part that comes from close pairs.

G4 is not met. T1 fails on the melt, NVE drift was not compared with M6, and there is no speed measurement at `N ≥ 256k`. The prototype stays off the default path. A negative result here is the planned outcome when the tolerance is missed.
