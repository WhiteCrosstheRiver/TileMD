# M8 — Pair-attention API and EAM

## What is in

`EvaluateAttention` walks a full pair list once per pass. `EvaluateAttentionTiles` walks the same passes on the cluster-pair list. For each ordered pair the potential returns a force scalar `s`, a pair energy, and a value that is summed onto the i atom. The force is rebuilt as `r_i (Σ s) − Σ s r_j`, using the periodic image of `r_j`. `AtomUpdate` runs after the pass, so the next pass can read what it stored.

Lennard-Jones is one pass. The scalar is the same `s(r²)` as the double-precision oracle, including the energy and force shifts. EAM is two passes on a funcfl table. Pass 0 sums the electron density. Pass 1 uses `s = −[(F'(ρ_i) + F'(ρ_j)) ρ'(r) + φ'(r)] / r`. The table is splined with the LAMMPS cubic used by `pair_style eam`, and `r φ(r)` is `27.2 × 0.529 × Z(r)²`. Distances are Angstrom and energies are eV. The Lennard-Jones fixtures stay in reduced units.

## What was run

`BUILD_DIR=build-m8 bash scripts/check.sh` passes the CPU suite.

On `fcc_500`, `boundary_500`, `cutoff_500`, `twotype_2000`, and `melt_4000`, the Lennard-Jones path matches the oracle. Where the reference force is not round-off, the relative force error is below `1e-10`, and the energy and virial agree at the same level. `fcc_500` is checked by absolute residual because its net force is numerical noise.

Cu with `tests/potentials/Cu_u3.eam` (Foiles, the LAMMPS funcfl) on a 3×3×3 fcc cell, one atom displaced, against LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 Update 4, `pair_style eam`, metal units, 1 OpenMP thread: relative force error `1.45e-13`. Both total energies are `-382.082246` eV. A four-atom finite difference of that energy matches the force component to the test tolerance. The reference rows are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cu_u3_108.txt`.

The same cell then runs 100 velocity-Verlet steps at `dt = 0.001` ps. Metal units accelerate with LAMMPS's factor `1 / 1.0364269e-4`. Against `fix nve` the largest position difference is `1.3e-14` Å and the largest velocity difference is `2.4e-13` Å/ps. Both final energies are `-381.720486` eV. The rows are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cu_u3_nve_100.txt`. The cluster-pair walk of the same 100 steps differs from that LAMMPS file by at most `1.35e-14` Å and `2.38e-13` Å/ps, and ends at the same energy.

`EvaluateAttentionTiles` on the five Lennard-Jones fixtures matches the oracle at the same force tolerance. On `boundary_500` the off-diagonal virial is compared with the largest virial component, because a few near contacts make the individual off-diagonal entries cancel. The copper EAM cell through that tile walk matches LAMMPS with relative force error `1.62e-13` and the same total energy, `-382.082246` eV.

`tilemd input.in` runs that metal Verlet on the cluster-pair list when the file says `units metal` and `eam`. The skin widens the list and does not change the cutoff. On the 108-atom copper cell, 100 steps at `dt = 0.001` ps match `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cu_u3_nve_100.txt` with a largest position difference of `1.35e-14` Å and a largest velocity difference of `2.38e-13` Å/ps. The mass `63.55` comes from the funcfl header. A structure file that stores velocities supplies them when the input has no `velocity` line. This path does not call the GPU.

The full-pair walk of that cell runs 10^5 steps (100 ps), with thermo every 100 steps. The input runner now uses the cluster list, so this series is the earlier full-pair record. LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 Update 4, `pair_style eam`, 1 OpenMP thread, uses the same positions, velocities, and timestep. The least-squares slope of total energy per atom versus time is `-1.939e-9` eV/atom/ps for both codes. The largest gap between the two energy series is `2.44e-13` eV/atom. Each series moves at most `1.39e-6` eV/atom away from its own initial energy. The samples are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cu_u3_nve_drift.txt`. `tests/validation/eam_nve_drift.py` writes the inputs.

`EamSetfl` reads a DYNAMO setfl file the way LAMMPS `pair_style eam/alloy` does: three comment lines, then each element's embedding and density, then `r phi(r)` for pairs with the first index at least the second. On four Cu-Ni atoms, the full list and the cluster-pair walk both match LAMMPS forces to a relative error of `1.5e-15`. The total energy is `-2.03585213` eV. The rows are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cuni_setfl_4.txt`. The table is the LAMMPS-shipped `CuNi.eam.alloy` (Onat and Durukanoglu, 2014). One hundred NVE steps at `dt = 0.001` ps, with Ni mass `58.689` and Cu mass `63.546`, stay within `6.2e-15` Å and `8.5e-15` Å/ps of LAMMPS on both the full list and the cluster-pair walk. The final energy is `-2.25069179` eV. The frame is `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cuni_setfl_nve_100.txt`. `tilemd input.in` takes the same setfl path: each atom is kicked with its element's mass, Ni `58.689` and Cu `63.546`, and the dumped step 100 matches that frame. A metal `velocity` line draws variance `k_B T / (m mvv2e)` for that mass and removes the mass-weighted momentum. Metal Langevin uses the same BAOAB split as the Lennard-Jones reference. The noise amplitude is `sqrt(k_B T (1 - e^{-2 γ dt}) / (m mvv2e))`. Zero friction reproduces the metal Verlet trajectory. Zero temperature multiplies a force-free velocity by `e^{-γ t}`. One thousand steps stay within `1.4e-13` Å. A 10^5-step energy series agrees with LAMMPS to `1.8e-14` eV/atom through step 3000. After that the four-atom gas separates; the largest gap is `1.15e-4` eV/atom. The samples are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/cuni_setfl_nve_drift.txt`. `tests/validation/eam_setfl_drift.py` writes the inputs.

`RunLjAttentionGpu` walks the host-built cluster list with one warp per i-cluster, the same lane layout as the M5 tile kernel. Pair coordinates are the double-precision slot positions. The owning lane reconstructs `r_i (Σ s) − Σ s r_j` and the host sums energy and virial in atom order. A host replay of that lane map, including the four-way reduction, matches the oracle on `fcc_500`, `boundary_500`, `cutoff_500`, `twotype_2000`, and `melt_4000` at the same `1e-10` force tolerance. `BUILD_DIR=build-m8` compiles the device kernel into `tilemd_gpu_tests` as `GpuLj.AttentionKernelMatchesOracle`. That launch has not been run: the 32k drift still occupies the only GPU, so the device force comparison and the G5 overhead number are not measured.

## Gate
