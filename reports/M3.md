# M3 — GPU v0

## What was built

`src/md/engine.cu` is the conventional engine from PLAN.md section 7, milestone M3: a thread-per-atom neighbor list, an LJ force, and velocity Verlet. Atom arrays stay in input order. The cell list only orders the neighbor build. After the build, neighbors are insertion-sorted by atom index so the pair order matches the CPU oracle.

Positions and velocities are double. The neighbor search uses a wrapped float shadow. The force kernel evaluates pairs in double and stores float forces. Energy and virial are one host running total over the sorted list. Each step may sync with the host. Details are in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

## Machine

NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050, compute capability 8.6, 6144 MiB. The binary targets `sm_80` and `sm_90` and runs this card by JIT from the `sm_80` PTX image. `gpumd` is not on `PATH`. LAMMPS is 22 Jul 2025 Update 4 at `C:\ProgramData\anaconda3\Scripts\lmp.exe`, without a KOKKOS package. There is no H100 on this machine.

T3 uses `tests/validation/lammps_ref/in.melt_32k` and the step-0 dump `init_32k.dump` (LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 Update 4, one CPU thread, `pair_modify shift no`). Thermo `pe` and `ke` are per atom. TileMD samples the same steps. The run took 114 s, about `2.8e6` atom-steps/s. Means over the 11 samples:

T7 cannot use `fcc_500` with skin 0.3: the box length is 8.40 and the padded reach is about 2.80, so `floor(L / reach)` is 2 and the engine rejects the box. The dynamics tests use `melt_4000` (`L = 16.8`, 6 cells).

## Virial association

`boundary_500` has 16 302 ordered pairs inside `r_c`. The padded neighbor list has 16 304 slots. The two extras have `r^2 = 6.2511` against `r_c^2 = 6.25` and are skipped by the exclusive cutoff test. The accepted set has no missing pairs and no duplicates.

Summing each atom's virial partial, then summing the partials, produced `xz = 247544426725.12646` against the oracle `247540454320.74548` (absolute difference `3972404.38`, relative `1.60e-5`). A single running total over the same pairs, in atom order and then neighbor-index order, reproduces the oracle value. The published energy and virial use that single total. Compensated summation of the partials does not recover it: the low bits are already gone from each partial.

## Deviations

- The force polynomial uses double coordinates. Float geometry on `melt_4000` left a relative force error near `1e-3`, outside the `1e-5` gate.
- `fcc_500` is compared with the plan's absolute bound. Its reference rms is about `1e-14`, so the relative metric is not meaningful.
- Energy and virial are reduced on the host. The force kernel still writes per-atom scratch.
- No spatial sort, no CUDA graphs, no device-side rebuild decision.

## Drift

T4 uses the M3 binary in `build/`. Both runs are the 32k melt from `init_32k.dump`, `lj/sf` with cutoff 2.5, skin 0.3, dt = 0.005, and 10^6 NVE steps. Thermo is every 1 000 steps, 1 001 samples, from time 0 to 5 000. LAMMPS is 22 Jul 2025 Update 4, one OpenMP thread, `thermo_modify format float %.16g`. Its total energy per atom is the `etotal` column. The TileMD file was written with the default six significant digits, and the full-precision slope printed by that process was not kept. The slope below is the least-squares fit of the recorded samples. The rows are `tests/validation/lammps_ref/drift_32k.txt`.

| Series | Slope of E/N vs time | Standard error | Energy span |
|---|---|---|---|
| TileMD | −3.545e-10 | 2.354e-10 | 6.0e-5 |
| LAMMPS | +1.521e-10 | 2.245e-10 | 6.3e-5 |

The absolute ratio is 2.33, so TileMD is not within 2× of the LAMMPS slope. Both slopes are inside two standard errors of zero: the energy wanders by about 1e-5, and the fitted trend over 5 000 τ is smaller than that wander. The endpoint change divided by 5 000 is −4.0e-9 for the recorded TileMD samples and −7.0e-9 for LAMMPS. `tilemd_long_run` now writes 17 significant digits. This comparison uses the six-digit file that the finished run actually wrote.
